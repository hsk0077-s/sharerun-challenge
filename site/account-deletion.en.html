ShareRun account deletion

App: ShareRun · Operator: 하상기

Delete directly in the app

  1. Open ShareRun and sign in.
  2. Open My from the bottom tabs and go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the screen and confirm following the instructions.

If you cannot use the app (request on the web)

Send the following to src791004@gmail.com. We process it after verifying your identity.

  • Subject: ShareRun account deletion request
  • The sign-in service you used (Google, Kakao, Naver) and the nickname shown in the app

We process requests within 10 days.

What happens when you delete?

Handled immediatelyCleaned up over time
Your sign-in account is deleted.
DIA, SHARE and VALUE balances become 0 and cannot be restored.
The push notification value is deleted.
The link is released so you can sign up again with the same Kakao or Naver account.		 Activity records such as running records, daily step records and notification records are deleted within 30 days.
Payment and transaction records that must be kept by law are kept for the required period and then deleted. The period is 5 years.

If you want a refund for unused paid DIA you purchased, you can apply only within 7 days of purchase, so please apply on the app's refund screen before deleting.
