ShareRun Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-25 · Last updated: 2026-10-24

하상기 (the "Operator") handles users' personal information with care while providing ShareRun (the "App"), and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws. The Operator processes only the minimum personal information needed to provide the service. This policy explains what information the Operator collects and why, how it is used and kept, and what rights you have.

2. Retention period

  • Account information and activity records: until you delete the account. When you delete, it is handled as described under "Account deletion" below.
  • Appeal evidence photos: immediately upon decision, or 14 days if there is no decision.
  • Payment and currency transaction records (records on contracts or withdrawal of subscription): kept for 5 years under Article 6(1)2 of the Enforcement Decree of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce.

Destruction procedure and method. Personal information is destroyed without delay once its retention period ends or its processing purpose is achieved. When you delete your account, the sign-in account and push notification value are deleted immediately, the currency balance is set to 0, and the account status is changed to "deleted". Activity records such as running records are deleted from the database by a cleanup job that handles deleted accounts together. It is completed within 30 days after account deletion. Payment and transaction records that must be preserved by law are kept separately from other information (the legal basis and period are stated under "2. Retention period" above) and are deleted when the retention period ends. Electronic files are destroyed by deleting them from the database.

4. Your rights

You can at any time view, correct or delete your personal information, or request that its processing be stopped. You can change consent items on the app's "My" screen and delete your account through "My → Settings → Delete account". If you cannot use the app, make your request at the contact below. Requests are handled within 10 days.

For the detailed deletion procedure, see the account deletion guide. For the conditions of using the service, see the Terms of Service.

5. Children's personal information

Children under 14 years of age cannot sign up. At sign-up you are asked to confirm that you are 14 or older, and the time of confirmation is recorded (your date of birth is not collected).

6. Automated decisions

Without human involvement, the Operator uses an automated verification system (a rule-based system that decides by set criteria and does not learn from your information) to decide whether a running record is recognized and whether a reward (such as VALUE) is granted accordingly (Article 37-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act).

  • Subjects and purpose: users who submit running records. This is to filter out records made on scooters, bicycles or vehicles, or records that cannot be regarded as walking or running, and to give rewards only for records you ran yourself.
  • Information used and how decisions are made: distance, time, average speed, speed by route segment, step count and stride, movement (gyro) stability, device integrity check results, and (if you consent) heart rate and cadence. A record that meets the criteria is recognized; one that appears to be made on a means of transport, or whose values do not match each other, is not recognized and no reward is granted. The detailed criteria are not disclosed in order to prevent misuse.
  • Sensitive information: heart rate is used only if you separately consent. Information of children under 14 is not processed.
  • Refusal and requesting an explanation: if you believe a record was wrongly rejected, you can submit an explanation and an evidence photo on the app's appeal (objection) screen, or ask by the email below for an explanation of the reason for the decision and a review. The Operator reviews appeals in person again and tells you the result.

7. How personal information is kept safe

  • All communication is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Currency, reward and sponsorship amounts are changed only on the server; the app does not write the values directly.
  • Staff who can access personal information are limited, and administrator access (such as appeal evidence) is logged.

We do not promise perfect security, but we make efforts to protect personal information through the measures above.
